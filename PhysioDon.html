About
the Physio

One therapist · Every session

I treat every patient myself. There is no rotating staff.

I set up PHYSIO DON because I was tired of watching people get handed a sheet of exercises and sent out the door. Recovery doesn’t work like that. It works when somebody watches you move, corrects you on the spot, and keeps adjusting the plan week by week until the pain is actually gone.

Most of the people who walk in here have been living with the problem for months — a back that won’t settle, a knee that buckles on stairs, a shoulder frozen since last winter. We start with a proper assessment, I explain what I find in plain language, and we get to work. No jargon, no upselling, no endless package deals.

If I think you need an orthopaedic opinion or a scan first, I’ll tell you that rather than waste your time and money. That’s the whole promise.

How It
Works

Three steps. No packages.
1

Call or WhatsApp

Tell me what hurts and since when. Two minutes on the phone is usually enough for me to say whether physiotherapy is the right first move, or whether you should see a doctor before we start.

[+91 phone]

2

Assessment

First session is a proper look at how you move — strength, range, the specific movements that reproduce your pain. I check your reports if you have any, and I explain what I find in words you can repeat to your family.

3

Personalised Plan

Hands-on treatment in the clinic, plus a short home routine — short meaning under ten minutes, not a full-page menu. We review it every session and change it as you get stronger. You’ll always know where you stand.
