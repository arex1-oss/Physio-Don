About
the Physio

One therapist · Every session

A thoughtful, movement-focused approach to everyday recovery.

PHYSIO DON is presented as a neighbourhood physiotherapy service for people in Phoolbagan, Beliaghata and nearby parts of Kolkata. The focus is on understanding what is limiting your movement and discussing a practical care plan before treatment begins.

Your first visit can include a conversation about your symptoms, relevant medical history and day-to-day activities, followed by an assessment appropriate to your needs. You can ask questions and understand the proposed next steps in clear language.

Treatment recommendations should be individualised. If your symptoms need medical review or further investigation, seek advice from an appropriately qualified clinician before proceeding with a rehabilitation plan.

How It
Works

Three steps. No packages.
1

Call or WhatsApp

Tell me what hurts and since when. Two minutes on the phone is usually enough for me to say whether physiotherapy is the right first move, or whether you should see a doctor before we start.

Contact details to be confirmed

2

Assessment

First session is a proper look at how you move — strength, range, the specific movements that reproduce your pain. I check your reports if you have any, and I explain what I find in words you can repeat to your family.

3

Personalised Plan

Hands-on treatment in the clinic, plus a short home routine — short meaning under ten minutes, not a full-page menu. We review it every session and change it as you get stronger. You’ll always know where you stand.

Common
Questions

Before you get started
What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any relevant prescriptions, scan reports or discharge summaries you already have, along with a list of medicines if applicable. Do not delay urgent medical care to arrange a physiotherapy appointment.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

Requirements can depend on your condition and local clinical advice. If you have severe, sudden or unexplained symptoms, consult a qualified medical practitioner first.

Can I ask about home visits?

Ask the clinic directly whether home visits are currently offered in your neighbourhood and what appointment slots may be available.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on your condition, assessment findings, goals and response to care. A suitable clinician should discuss an individual plan rather than promise a fixed number of sessions.
